## Lore
* Someone creates a website where they blog about computer science topics regularly
* They start exploring concepts like neural networks, networking protocols, and procedural page generation
* They find some patterns that don't make sense and investigate
* They find that behind all of the computer networking is some system that is systematically catagorizing all data
  * Maybe it was created by a benevolent programmer trying to index the web
* The blogger calls it "The Archive"
* The archive is finding patterns in the data much better than any human could
* Any data that it predicts is out of line gets removed
* 
* Some of their code starts getting corrupted and they have to restore code from backups
  * They research why their code gets corrupted

### Lore Timeline
1999
* Someone proposes an algorithm that crawls the web looking for data
* All of the data gets stored in a decentralized database
* To fit all data in existence in a decentralized database, a new format needs to be developed
* The programmer is rejected for the idea

2002
* 3 years later, the same programmer brings up the topic in a private conversation
* The person he talks about it with at first rejects it, but later starts to consider it a possibility
* After four months of research, work, and programming, they have built an algorithm that intelligently compresses data
  * The algorithm creates a compression format based on its context, the data, and everything else
  * No human could possibly understand how it works, especially becasuse it constantly changes
* He goes back to the original programmer and tells them about this new algorithm
* He scambles to create the database and crawler using this new algorithm
  * He is worried that people will ridicule hime again, and so before releasing what he find, he decides to make it difficult to take down
  * He decides that if people want him to take it down, its better if he physically can not
* After deciding to wait until it produces results, the programmer forgets about this crazy idea
* The crawler continues working on the web, archiving all new data produced for years, waiting for a signal

2002-2019
* The Archive exists on the internet with no central server or database
* It slowly and meticulously crawls every part of the internet that is linked to and stores it all
* The Archive stores its findings in strange mediums and formats
* All of its findings are in the form of patterns
* The patterns and all of the data is stored in super-humanly compressed formats that no one understands but the Archive
* If it detects that it could be taken over by anything it flags the pattern as out of place
* If any patterns are out of place, the Archive tries to subtly force them back into place or destroy them

2018-2020
* An unassuming programmer starts a blog about computer science and tech
* He starts by writing about python and some projects he is making
* When a pandemic hits, he starts learning about neural networks
* Soon, he takes an interest in the web, old internet protocols, and 1900s era computer science
* He starts working on a crawler based on internet protocols and do frequent devlogs about the project
* After reading pages and pages of documentation he is still confused why the crawlers seem to disapear into the web
* Eventually he notices that some blog pages get 404ed or edited, seemingly randomly
* Slowly, with his knowledge of the internet and intelligent algorithms, he starts to peice together what is happening

* Before he starts, he creates a dead man's switch, not linked to from the main web and so offline from the Archive
* He spends day after day, month after month trying to get a glimse of the Archive
* Eventually, one day, the Archive slips up and he gets in
* A sinlge one of his many crawlers gets back to safety and returns a small bit of the compressed data and patterns, as well as some data that was in the proccess of being archived
* With this, he is able to guess at what the Archive is trying to do and how it does it 

2021
* After a year break from blogging, he comes back with a final blog
* The page gives very human clues for instructions on how to reach the MUD

* In the MUD is a locked (uneditable) page where the blogger describes his entire experience with the Archive
